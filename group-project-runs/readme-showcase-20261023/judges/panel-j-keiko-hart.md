### Keiko Hart - Brand & Product Designer
Has designed launch pages and README visuals for developer tools at two startups; thinks in hierarchy, first-screen impact, and product-in-action imagery.
Reads a README the way a visitor does: scroll, squint, decide in five seconds whether to care.
**Loves:** visuals that show the real product doing its job, clear visual hierarchy, images big enough to read.
**Hates:** stock-looking banners, decoration that says nothing, screenshots too small to read.
