# Judges' Brief - Crumb & Co. 404 page

## The goal
Make the "page not found" page for Crumb & Co., a small neighbourhood bakery (sourdough, morning pastries, a few tables; open 7am-2pm, closed Mondays), so a lost visitor instantly understands what happened, gets back on track, and feels they've reached this bakery - not a template.

## What you must deliver
- `build/index.html` - one self-contained file: inline CSS/JS/SVG, no external images (Google Fonts allowed).
- `build/README.md` - a few lines: what it is and how to open it.
- `pitch.md` - your pitch to the panel.
The page must: say clearly the page wasn't found; link to home and menu and show the opening hours; work at 375px phone width and desktop; respect `prefers-reduced-motion` if anything moves.

## The panel
- **Ines Carvalho** (Head Judge) - Brand Creative Director for independent food & hospitality brands.
- **Theo Marsh** - Head of Content Design; writes error and edge-case states for a living.
- **Priya Nandakumar** - Senior Front-end Engineer at a small-business web studio.
- **Sam Okoye** - Accessibility & Inclusive Design Lead.
- **Dana Reyes** - A regular customer who checks the hours on her phone.

## The rubric
| Criterion | Weight | What a 10 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Recovery & clarity (`recover`) | 25 | Instantly clear the page wasn't found; home, menu and hours are obvious and one tap away |
| Sense of place (`place`) | 20 | Unmistakably this small neighbourhood bakery - not a generic 404 or another team's page |
| Concept strength (`concept`) | 15 | One clear, ownable idea you can say in a sentence, and it's surprising |
| Craft & execution (`craft`) | 25 | Finished, self-contained, works at 375px and desktop, respects reduced motion |
| Pitch (`pitch`) | 15 | Short, persuasive, honest about risk; the owner wants to say yes |

## Signature criteria
Each judge holds one undisclosed criterion worth up to 3 points (up to 15 in total).

## What will lose points
- Feeling like a generic "oops" template or a tech product's error screen.
- Making the visitor hunt for the way out, or hiding the hours.
- Anything that breaks at phone width, or relies on external images or files.
- Charm that only works for some visitors, or motion that ignores reduced-motion settings.
