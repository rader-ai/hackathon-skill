# Sealed - j-sam-okoye
**Name:** Keyboard path home
**Description:** Using only a keyboard (and a screen reader), can a visitor reach and activate the link back home in three Tab presses or fewer, with a clearly visible focus indicator and sensible link names?
**Scoring 0-10:** 0 = unreachable or invisible focus; 5 = reachable but focus faint, order odd, or links named "click here"; 10 = home in 1-3 Tabs, strong visible focus, every link name makes sense out of context.
**Why he cares:** Lost visitors who navigate by keyboard or screen reader are the most lost of all; the way out must be the first thing they hit.
