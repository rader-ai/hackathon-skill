# Sealed - j-theo-marsh
**Name:** Ten-second read
**Description:** Can every word of visible copy on the first screen be read in about ten seconds, with nothing cuttable? Measures copy economy and discipline, not tone or brand.
**Scoring 0-10:** 0 = paragraphs, filler, repeated ideas; 5 = right message but 30-50% could go; 10 = I can't remove a word without losing meaning.
**Why he cares:** Error states are read by people who are already slightly annoyed. Every extra word is a tax on them.
