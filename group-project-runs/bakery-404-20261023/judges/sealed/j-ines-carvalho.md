# Sealed - j-ines-carvalho
**Name:** From screen to doorstep
**Description:** Does the page leave a lost web visitor actually wanting to walk into the shop - with a concrete, real-world reason and enough practical info (when it's open, where) to act on it today?
**Scoring 0-10:** 0 = purely an online dead end; 5 = mentions the shop but gives no pull to visit; 10 = I finish the page planning when I'll go and what I'll get.
**Why she cares:** A neighbourhood bakery's website exists to fill the shop. A 404 that only routes clicks wastes the one moment a stranger was thinking about them.
