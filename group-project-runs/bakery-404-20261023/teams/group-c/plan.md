# Group C plan (Ben, Producer)

## Direction
The Bake Sheet: the missing page is a line the baker looked for on today's pencilled bake sheet and crossed off, and the sheet's time grid (7am to 2pm, Monday struck out) is the way back.

## Why it beats the alternatives (rubric)
- recover (25): plain H1 "Page not found" leads; Home, Menu, Hours are three 48px rows; hours printed on the sheet itself, never hidden.
- place (20): a bake sheet is a bakery-only object; its columns are Crumb & Co's real day. Slip and Bag Note would fit a florist.
- concept (15): one sentence, ownable, not a pun: "the missing page is a crossed-off line on the bake sheet."
- craft (25): one file, plain-face body, handwriting as accent, 375px first, reduced-motion safe.
- pitch (15): the owner recognises her own morning routine.

## Rejected, dissent
- Ada's Slip: any shop's receipt. Ada conceded in 2a.
- My Bag Note: staple and tick-boxes are props, and the open-now clock lies across timezones (Ada's point, accepted).
- Noor's loaves and flour puff: the mascot Ada kills, and blurry at 375px. Noor conceded.
- Dissent: Ada allows one wink only, no headline pun. Joe's twee risk noted.

## Build exactly (Joe, 4 min, build/index.html + build/README.md only)
1. H1 "Page not found" in a plain face (system serif/sans). One lettered line beneath in a Google handwriting font with system cursive fallback: "Not on today's sheet."
2. Mistyped path from location.pathname, set via textContent (escaped), word-break so long URLs wrap, penciled with one thick SVG strike stroke drawing once. Text also states it; strike is not the only signal.
3. Lined-paper CSS, cream, no loaves.
4. Three 48px+ full-width rows: Home (the front counter, /), Menu (what's in the oven, /menu), Hours.
5. Hours grid: Mon struck "no bake", Tue-Sun 7am-2pm, today ringed in pencil. Line: "Closed Mondays, back Tuesday at 7" shown on Mondays only; no open-now claim (timezone).
6. One plain dry sentence from the baker, signed. Margin note: "tell us if a link sent you here."
7. prefers-reduced-motion: strike static. AA contrast.
8. Check: open at 375px and desktop once.

## Cut to fit
Hand-lettered SVG heading, riso loaves, flour puff, open-now/closed live logic, tick-boxes, bag/staple, extra doodles.
