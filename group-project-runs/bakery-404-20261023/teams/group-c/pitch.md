# The Bake Sheet - Group C (Noor)

**Hook: Your missing page is just a line we crossed off this morning's bake sheet.**

## The idea
Every bakery runs on a pencilled sheet by the oven. A visitor on a dead link is told honestly that we looked for that line and it isn't on today's sheet. The sheet's own week grid, 7am to 2pm with Monday struck out, is the way back.

## What the build does
One self-contained index.html on ruled cream paper. A plain "Page not found" headline, a handwritten "Not on today's sheet," and "Open 7am-2pm, closed Mondays" right beneath. The mistyped path is struck through by a pencil stroke that draws once. Three full-width rows of at least 48px: Home, Menu, Full week. The hours table rings today and says "(today)"; Monday reads "no bake". Stroke animation stops under reduced-motion.

## Why it wins
- **Recovery:** clear headline, hours above the fold, big tap targets.
- **Place:** a bake sheet belongs to a bakery; copy names sourdough, a few tables, coffee.
- **Concept:** one sentence, no pun.
- **Craft:** one file, inline CSS/JS/SVG, caption and row headers on the table.
- **Pitch:** the owner recognises her own morning.

## Honest risk
- Home and Menu link to "/" and "/menu", which are guesses.
- Caveat font needs network; offline falls back to system cursive.
- Our skeptic found it reads more stationery than bakery; we added a small loaf and the sourdough line.
- The 375px layout is not browser-verified.
- Cut: hand-lettered heading, riso loaves, flour puff, live open-now logic, tick-boxes.

*Come Tuesday. The bread is baked.*
