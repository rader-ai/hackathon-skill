# Noor's proposal (Group C, Humanists)

## The three obvious ideas (not mine)
1. A burnt-toast / sad-croissant mascot saying "Oops, we burned this page."
2. "Sold out" - the page is gone like the last baguette, with bread puns.
3. A "lost in the crumbs" trail leading to a big Home button.

## Mine: The Bake Sheet
**Concept:** The missing page is one crossed-out line on the baker's pencil bake sheet, with a handwritten note under it telling you where to go.

**Looks/feels:** Flour-smudged, cream riso-style paper, tidy columns in handwriting font. A line reads "/the-address-you-typed - not on today's sheet" struck through in pencil. Below, the baker's note: "Couldn't find that page. Here's what we *did* bake." Three big tap rows: Home, Menu, Hours (7am-2pm, closed Mondays; today highlighted in pencil circle). A few hand-drawn SVG loaves in two-colour print. A flour puff on the strike-through, disabled under reduced motion.

**Rubric:**
- recover: Plain sentence "We couldn't find that page" up top; three full-width tap rows; hours printed, not hidden. 9.
- place: Bake sheets exist only in bakeries; first-person baker voice. 8.
- concept: Sayable in one sentence, unexpected, not a pun. 8.
- craft: One file, system-safe handwriting fallback, large type at 375px. 8.
- pitch: Owner recognises her own sheet. 8.

**Riskiest part:** Handwriting font and fine pencil detail blurring on a phone, and the cute reading as twee. Mitigation: big strokes, body text in a legible face, handwriting only for accents.
