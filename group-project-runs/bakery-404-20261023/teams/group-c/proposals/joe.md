# Joe - Proposal

## Three obvious ideas
1. A cute loaf/croissant mascot with a sad face and a bread pun ("This page is toast").
2. A "sold out" chalkboard sign: "Page sold out, try the menu".
3. A trail of crumbs leading back home (animated).

## Mine: The Bake Sheet
**Concept:** The 404 is the baker's pencilled bake sheet, and the address you typed is a line she looked for and crossed off: "not on today's sheet".

**Looks/feels:** Cream lined paper with flour smudges, hand-lettered SVG heading ("Not on today's sheet"), your mistyped path (read live from the URL) penciled in with a hand-drawn strike-through. Below, the sheet's real lines are the exits: "Home - the front counter", "Menu - what's in the oven", "Hours - 7am-2pm, closed Mondays", each a big tap target. A tiny margin note in the baker's hand: "Sorry - tell us if a link sent you here." A today-aware line ("Closed Mondays - back Tuesday at 7") computed from the clock. The pencil stroke draws once; disabled under reduced motion.

**Rubric:**
- recover: plain H1 "Page not found" plus the lettered line; three huge links; hours always visible, no hunting.
- place: bake-sheet vocabulary (proof, bake, bench) is only a bakery's; the baker's voice.
- concept: "the missing page is a crossed-off line on the bake sheet" - one sentence, ownable.
- craft: single file, inline SVG, system+Google font fallback, 375px-first, 44px targets, AA contrast, reduced-motion respected.
- pitch: owner recognises her own morning routine.

**Riskiest part:** Hand-lettering via SVG/fonts must stay legible at 375px, and the cute framing must not bury the plain "not found" message. Also the crossed-out URL must not be the only signal (text states it too).
