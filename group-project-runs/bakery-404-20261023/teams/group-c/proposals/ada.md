# Ada (Skeptic) - Proposal

## The three obvious ideas (I'm not doing these)
1. A sad or burnt croissant/toast mascot with "Oops, we're toast!"
2. Bread-pun headline ("Crumbs! This page has loafed off").
3. The empty pastry case: "Sold out" as the 404 metaphor.

## Mine: The Baker's Slip
**Concept:** The 404 is the paper slip the baker scribbles when you ask for something she doesn't have: your mistyped URL written in pencil, struck through, with her three corrections underneath.

**Looks/feels:** Kraft-paper tone, one off-white slip, slightly rotated, set in a single serif plus a handwriting face for her pencil. No mascot, no illustration beyond a ruled line and a strike-through. Pencil text reads like a person: "Not on the shelf. Try these." Below, three plain written lines: Home, Menu, and "Open 7-2, closed Mondays" in full view. Strike-through draws once; static under reduced motion.

**Rubric:**
- recover: "Page not found" in the first line; three large tap targets; hours never hidden. Strong.
- place: Slip, pencil, kraft, closing time. Specific, not decorated. Good.
- concept: Sayable in one sentence, but quiet. Medium-high.
- craft: Almost no assets; trivially fine at 375px, contrast-safe, nothing relies on motion. Strong.
- pitch: Owner can picture it at the till. Honest about restraint.

**Riskiest part:** Restraint reads as bland. If the handwriting and wording aren't truly the baker's, it's just a beige box. It must be funny once, in a plain sentence, and no more.
