# Ben (Producer) - Proposal

**Three most obvious ideas (avoiding all):**
1. Burnt-toast / "we've burnt this page" pun page.
2. A lost croissant or mascot wandering a blank screen.
3. A "sold out" empty bakery display case.

**Mine: "The Bag Note"**
**Concept:** The 404 is the pencilled note a baker staples to a paper bread bag when an order can't be filled: "Couldn't find that one - here's what you probably wanted," with three tick-boxes you can actually tap.

**Looks/feels:** Kraft-paper bag on a warm cream table, a stapled white order slip in handwriting font, one pencil-ticked line each: Home, Menu, Hours. Hours sit on the slip like the shop-door sign (7-2, closed Mondays), with "open now / closed today" worked out from the visitor's clock. The only motion is the pencil tick drawing itself once; off under reduced-motion.

**Rubric:**
- recover: "Page not found" in plain words at the top, three big tap-box links, hours on the slip. Very strong.
- place: Bag, staple, flour smudge, baker's voice (first person, a name signed at the bottom). Strong.
- concept: Sayable in one sentence, nobody else's 404 is a bakery order slip. Good.
- craft: Pure CSS/SVG, one file, big targets, real text for screen readers. Strong.
- pitch: Owner can picture it on a real bag. Good.

**Riskiest part:** The handwriting font's legibility and contrast on kraft at 375px; fallback is a plainer face for the hours.
