## Round 1 - Noor (Visionary)

Everyone else will reach for the burnt toast or the sold-out loaf. I keep thinking about the bake sheet taped up by the oven at 4am: pencil, flour, crossed-out lines. What if the missing page is just one of those lines, struck through, and the baker has scribbled underneath what to do instead? It sounds like her, not like us. Hours sit right there as the day's schedule, with the closed Monday ringed.

I know my blind spot: fine pencil texture turns to mush on a phone. So Joe, please keep strokes fat and let Ada hold me to plain words, no puns. Ben, make the three taps huge. I'd rather lose a smudge than lose the way out.
