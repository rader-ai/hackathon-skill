## Round 1 - Rina (Producer)

I'll be blunt: a lost visitor has one question, "are they open?", and every 404 on earth answers a different one. So we flip it. Hours lead, live from the visitor's clock. The missing page becomes a stamped line on a kitchen ticket, plain and unmissable, just not the hero.

Three obvious ideas are off the table: crumb puns, burnt toast, sold-out chalkboard. Ticket is not any of them.

My worry is mood. I optimise clicks and forget warmth, so I need Tove for texture (paper, stamp, grain) and Sam to confirm the 404 line stays unmistakable. Vision gets the voice. I get the three tap targets above the fold at 375px. Ship small, ship finished.
