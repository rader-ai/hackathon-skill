# Kit - Proposal

## Three obvious ideas (rejected)
1. Sad/crumbling croissant illustration with a "Oops, we're toast!" pun.
2. "Sold out" chalkboard: the page is out of stock.
3. Out-of-the-oven metaphor: "this page got burnt", animated oven.

## Mine: "The Hours Come First"
**Concept:** A 404 that breaks the convention of leading with the error: it leads with the bakery's live status ("Open till 2pm. Come in." or "Closed Mondays. Back Tuesday, 7am."), and the missing page is just a small till-receipt line underneath.

**Looks/feels:** Huge flour-white type on warm crust-brown, one hand-drawn loaf SVG. The status is computed from the visitor's clock. Beneath it, a torn receipt: "1 x page (/whatever-they-typed) ... not found. No charge." Three big buttons: Home, Menu, Hours. No pun, no apology.

**Rubric:**
- recover: 9. Hours are the hero, error stated plainly in receipt, three large links.
- place: 8. Live open/closed state, receipt, loaf, Monday rule - only this shop.
- concept: 8. Sayable in one sentence; flips the genre.
- craft: 8. Small inline JS, SVG, no motion needed (or one gentle steam wisp, reduced-motion safe).
- pitch: 8. "Your visitor was probably lost on the way to buy bread; tell them if you're open."

**Riskiest part:** The error being demoted could read as unclear. Mitigation: an h1 "Page not found" stays visible, just smaller than the status. Also timezone: show hours text always, live line is a bonus.
