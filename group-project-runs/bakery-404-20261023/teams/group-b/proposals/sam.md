# Sam (Skeptic) - Proposal

## The three obvious ideas
1. Burnt/missing-croissant illustration with "Oops, we're toast!" puns.
2. "Sold out" page: this page has sold out, with a tray of empty bread.
3. Chalkboard menu where the 404 sits above link-chips.

## Mine: "Probably Not Here For The Error"
**Concept:** The 404 is a footnote; the headline answers what a lost bakery visitor actually wants - are they open right now?

**Looks/feels:** Plain paper-bag-brown page, one big line in a sturdy serif: "Open until 2pm today." (or "Closed today. Back Tuesday 7am." on Mondays; "Opens at 7am" before opening). Computed from the visitor's clock, with a static full-week hours table underneath so it works without JS. Beneath: a single calm sentence, "That page isn't here (404)." Then three big links: Home, Menu, Find us. No mascot, no pun, no apology. Small hand-inked SVG loaf as the only decoration.

**Rubric:**
- recover: strongest play; hours first, error stated plainly, links 48px+.
- place: hours, Monday closure, sourdough details and a neighbourhood voice; moderate.
- concept: breaks "the error is the headline"; sayable in one sentence; surprising by restraint.
- craft: tiny file, no motion at all, so reduced-motion is trivially met; status is text, not colour.
- pitch: honest - it trades wit for usefulness.

**Riskiest part:** Too dry. Judges may find it correct but cold (my blind spot: I distrust wit). Also timezone: use a fixed bakery-local label ("Crumb & Co. time") and fall back to the static table.
