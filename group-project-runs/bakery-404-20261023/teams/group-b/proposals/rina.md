# Rina - Proposal

**Three obvious ideas (not mine):** (1) crumb-trail/"we've lost our crumbs" pun page; (2) burnt-toast or empty-plate illustration with "oops"; (3) "sold out" chalkboard for the missing page.

**Mine: The Order Ticket.** Concept: the 404 is a paper kitchen ticket where the *answer* leads and the error is the footnote: the biggest thing on the page is a live "Open now, till 2pm" / "Closed Mondays, back Tuesday 7am" line computed from the visitor's clock.

**Convention broken:** a 404 leads with the error. This one leads with what the lost visitor actually wants (are they open?), and the missing page is a rubber-stamped line, "Table for /whatever: not found", clear but second.

**Looks/feels:** cream ticket paper, torn edge, mono type, hand stamp, flour-dust grain. Three big tap rows: Home, Menu, Hours, ticked like order items. Only motion: stamp thumps once (off under reduced motion).

**Rubric:**
- Recovery 25: strongest possible; hours and 3 links above the fold at 375px.
- Place 20: ticket, hours and Monday closure are bakery-specific; no stock art.
- Concept 15: one sentence, surprising, ownable.
- Craft 25: tiny HTML/CSS, one inline script, easy to finish.
- Pitch 15: "fewer bounced visitors, fewer 'are you open?' calls."

**Riskiest part:** mood. Ticket may read as diner/printer, not sourdough warmth; and judges may read the small 404 line as unclear. Mitigation: stamp is large and rust-red, plain sentence, warm palette.
