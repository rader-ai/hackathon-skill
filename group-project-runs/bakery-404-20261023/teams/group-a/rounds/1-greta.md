## Round 1 - Greta (Skeptic)

I went looking for the obvious ideas and found three: crumb puns, a sad empty basket, a bread mascot. Every one makes the visitor read a joke before they find the exit. Nobody lands on a 404 wanting to be entertained; they want to know if the bakery is open and where the menu went.

So my pitch is "Open Right Now?": plain statement that the page is missing, then a live line from their clock about today's hours, then three big links. I'll defend this on evidence, not taste.

My honest worry: I mistake plain for good. If the room thinks this is bland, say so and tell me where. I'll accept one strong visual or voice detail, but I'll fight anything that pushes the links below the fold at 375px.
