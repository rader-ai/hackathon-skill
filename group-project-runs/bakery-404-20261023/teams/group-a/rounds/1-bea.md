## Round 1 - Bea (Visionary)

I threw out the croissant, the empty basket and the crumb trail. Everyone will draw those, and they're all stock "oops" energy.

What I keep thinking of is the little paper ticket at a busy counter. Your number is 404 and the counter doesn't have it. The page becomes that ticket, printed on cream stock with a perforated stub. The stub carries the hours, and three stamped tabs lead home, to the menu, and to the hours.

I know my weakness: I get lost in the nostalgia. So I'm asking Greta to hold the line on legibility and Omid to keep the words plain. If the texture makes anyone hunt for the exit, I'll cut the texture, not the exit.
