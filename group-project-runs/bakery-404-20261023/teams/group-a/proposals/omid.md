# Omid - Proposal

**Three obvious ideas:** (1) a burnt or dropped croissant with "Oops, crumbs!"; (2) a bread-pun 404 ("We loaf you, but..."); (3) an empty bakery case / "sold out" sign.

**Mine - The Daybook:** the 404 is a page of the bakery's handwritten baking ledger, where the missing URL is logged as an unfilled order and the day's actual bakes are listed beneath it.

**Looks/feels:** ruled cream ledger paper, ink-brown serif and one handwriting face for the entries. First line: "Not found: /whatever-they-typed" struck through in the ledger, with a plain note, "That page isn't on our books." Below, three large entries styled as ledger rows: Home, Menu, Hours (7am-2pm, closed Mondays; today's row highlighted by JS). Small flourish: the pen "writes" the struck line once, and is still under reduced-motion.

**Rubric:**
- Recover: plain headline, three big rows as tap targets, hours visible without clicking. High.
- Place: a family bakery's daybook, with flour-dust margin notes, is specific; no stock oops. High.
- Concept: one sentence, ownable, mildly surprising. Medium-high.
- Craft: single file, CSS only, fluid at 375px, motion optional. High.
- Pitch: "your 404 is a page from your own book."

**Riskiest part:** handwriting font and ruled-paper styling reading as nostalgic costume, and Bea wanting more ornament than clarity allows. Mitigation: type stays large, contrast AA.
