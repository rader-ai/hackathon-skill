# Luis - Proposal

**Three obvious ideas (skip these):**
1. Burnt toast / "we're toast" crumb puns.
2. Empty bread basket or sold-out chalkboard ("this page sold out").
3. Cute loaf mascot shrugging "oops".

**Mine: The Tally Stick.** Medieval bakers split a hazel stick and notched each sale, so both halves had to match. Concept: your link is a half-stick that has no partner in our records - the page you wanted has no matching notch.

**Looks/feels:** A CSS-drawn hazel tally stick across the page, warm paper background, hand-cut serif. Seven notches, one per weekday. Monday is left uncut (closed). Today's notch glows; "7am-2pm" is stamped on the stick itself. Headline in plain words: "We can't find that page." Three big buttons below: Home, Menu, Hours. Notches draw in once; static under reduced motion.

**Rubric:**
- recover: plain headline, three large buttons, hours readable as text, not only drawn.
- place: a baking trade object, with Monday visibly absent. No other shop would draw this.
- concept: "Your link has no matching notch" is sayable in one sentence and unexpected.
- craft: pure CSS/SVG, one file, stacks at 375px, motion gated by media query.
- pitch: the owner gets a wall-worthy object and a story for the shop.

**Riskiest part:** I over-build the drawing and the history reads as obscure. Mitigation: the plain-language line sits above the stick, and the stick is decoration plus the hours.
