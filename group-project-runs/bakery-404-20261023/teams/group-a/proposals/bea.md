# Bea's proposal

**Three obvious ideas (avoid):**
1. Pun-heavy "Oops, we've lost our crumbs" with a cartoon croissant.
2. "Sold out" empty bread basket illustration.
3. Crumb trail leading back home.

**Mine: The Ticket Stub.** The 404 is the bakery's paper queue ticket: "No. 404" is a number the counter doesn't have.

**Look/feel:** A cream letterpress trade card on kraft paper, slightly misregistered ink, a perforated stub edge. A brass-framed "Now serving" board reads 403... 405, with a gap where 404 should be. Plain headline: "We can't find that page." Beneath, three stamped tabs: Home, Menu, Hours (7am-2pm, closed Mondays, shown on the stub, with today's status). Serif display plus a typewriter numeral face. One motion: the ticket gently slides from the dispenser; disabled under reduced-motion.

**Rubric:**
- Recovery: headline is plain; three big tap targets; hours are printed on the ticket itself. 
- Place: counter culture of a neighbourhood shop, ink, paper, tin; nobody else will use a queue ticket.
- Concept: "You've drawn a number we don't have." Sayable in one sentence.
- Craft: pure CSS/SVG, one file, fluid to 375px, contrast checked.
- Pitch: we reuse the ticket motif for other states later.

**Riskiest part:** nostalgia swallowing function. If the textures or the "Now serving" joke get cute, the exit gets harder to see. Mitigation: links sit above the fold, high contrast, plain words.
