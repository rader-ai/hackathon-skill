# Greta - Proposal

## Three obvious ideas (not mine)
1. Pun-fest: "Oh crumbs! We've lost this page" with a crumb trail.
2. Empty bread basket / "sold out" illustration with a sad loaf.
3. Mascot loaf with big cartoon 404 digits made of bread.

## Mine: "Open Right Now?"
**Concept:** A 404 that answers the question most lost visitors actually have: is the bakery open, and where do I go?

**Looks/feels:** Warm cream paper, one plain serif sentence ("That page isn't here."), then a live line from the visitor's clock: "We're open until 2pm today" / "Opens 7am tomorrow" / "Closed Mondays, back Tuesday 7am". Three large buttons: Home, Menu, Hours. A single scored-loaf line drawing, quiet. No jokes in the headline.

**Rubric:**
- Recovery (25): strongest asset; status and links above the fold at 375px, 44px+ targets, no-JS fallback shows full hours.
- Place (20): moderate; relies on real hours and a small loaf drawing. Risk of feeling plain.
- Concept (15): clear one-liner, useful surprise, less "wow" than a visual gag.
- Craft (25): tiny JS, no motion needed; contrast AA; reduced-motion trivially satisfied.
- Pitch (15): honest: it trades spectacle for trust.

**Riskiest part:** plain can read as template. Needs one distinctive voice detail (handwritten chalk note about the sourdough) so restraint doesn't become blandness - my blind spot.
