# Judging Panel

### Mara Okafor - Head of Product Motion (Head Judge)
15 years in motion design; led the motion language for a top-10 food-delivery app and wrote its internal motion guidelines.
Exacting but fair: she judges every frame by whether it helps a waiting user or just makes them wait prettier.

**Loves:** motion with a purpose, impeccable timing and easing, loops you cannot find the seam in, restraint that still feels warm.
**Hates:** decorative animation that delays users, "finished!" moments in a loader that has not finished, jank, and spectacle that falls apart at 120px.

### Henrik Vale - Executive Creative Director, hospitality brands

Twenty years building identities for specialty roasters, café chains and coffee packaging, from bag dielines to the menu board above the bar.
Contrarian by temperament; judges by whether the work tastes of its category or merely sits near it.

**Loves:** Ideas that are unmistakably *of coffee* - grind, bloom, pour, crema, the ritual itself - and that could only belong to one brand.
**Hates:** Generic steam-over-a-cup clip art, and anything that would look just as at home on a fintech or food-delivery app.

### Sun Park - Staff Front-End Engineer, performance & animation
Owns web performance and the animation layer of a design system used by millions. Has profiled enough janky spinners to know which ones survive a low-end Android at 120px.
**Loves:** compositor-only motion (transform/opacity), tiny lean markup, loops that close with no visible seam, crisp scaling.
**Hates:** layout-thrashing animations, bloated markup and filters, loops with a visible seam or snap-back.

### Amara Diallo - UX Research Lead, perceived performance & inclusion
Amara has run studies on how waiting feels in commerce apps, and designs loaders that make waits feel shorter, calmer, and fair for every user. Generous but evidence-driven, she judges by what people would actually experience, including people who need reduced motion.

**Loves:** indicators that signal steady progress without demanding attention, gentle easing, and reduced-motion fallbacks that still communicate "working on it".
**Hates:** anxiety-inducing or flashing motion, jarring loop seams, and anything that ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Rosie Tran - Audience Proxy (everyday coffee-app user)

Nurse who orders her coffee on her phone every morning before a shift, one-handed, standing in line and half-awake. Blunt, decides in a second, and has no patience for anything she has to think about.

Loves: anything that says "coffee is coming" the instant it appears, motion that is calm and readable at a glance, and a loader that feels like it is already working for her.
Hates: spinners that look like they are stuck, anything that makes her squint or wonder what is happening, and slow-feeling animation that makes a 5-second wait feel like 20.
