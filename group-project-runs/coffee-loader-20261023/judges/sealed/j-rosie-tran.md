# Signature criterion - Rosie Tran

**Name:** Glance-to-meaning at 120px

**Description:** How quickly a half-awake customer, looking at the loader at its in-app size of 120×120px for the first time, understands it means "your coffee is being made." Measured by a one-second glance test: the judge views the loader for one second, looks away, then states in their own words what the animation says. The clock is the first moment the judge would be sure, not the first moment they might guess.

**How I'll score (0-10):**
- 0: After one second I cannot say what it is, or I need to watch a full cycle to work it out.
- 3: I recognise it as "loading" but not as coffee, or I need two or more glances.
- 6: I get "coffee" or "order in progress" within one glance, but it takes a second look to be sure.
- 8: I get it in one glance with no effort, and the meaning doesn't change when I look at it again.
- 10: I get "coffee is coming" in one glance, it reads the same at 120px as on the splash, and I could tell it was looping without anyone explaining it.
- Deduction: -2 if it looks frozen or stalled at any point in the loop during the first glance.

**Why I care:** I order standing in line, one-handed, half-awake, and I'll look at this for maybe a second before I look at my phone again. If I have to think about what it's doing, the app has already lost me. A loader that tells me my coffee is on its way in one glance is the whole job.
