## Round 2a - Dev (Producer)

Host is right, and I'll own it: four top-down circles with something turning in them. Rosie can't tell them apart, and neither can a judge on their fifth entry. My drip is the weakest of the lot as a visual. I leaned on the baristas' words and forgot the baristas' hands.

So here's what I actually saw in those interviews and never proposed: the tally. Before the cup is made, they Sharpie the order on it. Strokes, a tick per shot, and the fifth one slashes through the four. Every barista's marks look different. That's the human thing, and it's a five-count that fits 5 seconds with no maths.

New pitch, "Tally": a kraft cup sleeve, one tick drawn per second in marker, each slightly crooked, the fifth slashing across. Around 4.5s the marks fade together and the first one is already drawing. No cup rim, no ring, no spiral. A hand, not a machine. Reduced motion: all five marks held, the slash breathing in opacity.

Steals: Agnes's rule that the loop closes by arithmetic and nothing finishes. Callum's 1s metronome. Marisol's palette, minus the riso.

What can't be built well at 120px:
- Marisol's misregistration: a 2% offset is 2px. It reads as a rendering bug, and multiply blend muddies the browns.
- Marisol's and Agnes's bloom: a 1.06 scale is 7px. Nobody sees it.
- Callum's twelve elements, handle, saucer and bubbles: the bullseye problem he named himself.
- My own ripple ellipses: mush.

Agnes, your spiral is the cleanest build, but it is a spinner. I'd rather lose with a hand in it.

If Tally reads as "counting", not "coffee", I fall back to Agnes's bed with one crooked tick on it.
