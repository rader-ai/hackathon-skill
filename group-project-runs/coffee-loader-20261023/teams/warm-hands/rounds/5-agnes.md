## Round 5 - Agnes (Skeptic)

I went in hoping to hate it and mostly failed. The loop is clean, the file is 6KB, the colours are restrained, nothing finishes, and the reduced-motion state is a decision rather than a shrug. Annoying.

But here is what will cost us. First, there is no pitch.md. Fifteen points, gone, for forgetting a text file. Second, a spiral rotating rigidly around its own centre is not a pour. At 120px a tired nurse may see a cinnamon roll in a mug, and Henrik will say "near coffee". Give the stream a head, a fat sienna tip, so the eye follows a pour and not a decal, and say so in the pitch. Third, we claim compositor motion on SVG children that repaint; Sun will notice. Soften the claim or split the spiral into its own stacked svg.

Fourth, Callum's Safari worry is real. Kill transform-box entirely by moving the origin into the markup with translate groups, as written in redteam.md. Fifth, the "splash" is a pane, not the viewport. Sixth, the stain turns to hairline mush at 120.

The handle is cute. I am keeping it, under protest. Fix pitch.md first; then the tip; then the origin trick.
