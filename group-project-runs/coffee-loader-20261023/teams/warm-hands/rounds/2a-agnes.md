## Round 2a - Agnes (Skeptic)

Fatal flaws, one each, no discounts.

**Marisol's Bloom:** the riso misregistration is the whole warmth claim, and it is a static 2-3% offset. At 120px that is 3 pixels. Judges will read a rendering bug or nothing. Also "copper disc on brown disc" is a blob, as she admits.

**Dev's Drop:** a dripper over a cup is a side view. The rest of us are top-down. Fix the view or lose the ripples. And a one-second tick is a clock; Amara will hear it as a countdown.

**Callum's Pour:** expanding rings from a centre is a sonar ping, a Wi-Fi icon, a fintech "listening" state. The handle stub is doing all the coffee work, and it is a handle on a bullseye.

**Mine:** it is a spinner with good colours. I said so myself. The only defence is the bloom swell, and 6% scale is nearly invisible at 120px.

Host is right that none of us has done the human thing. Mine is small. A human pour is not constant: the wrist accelerates out of the turn, slows at the top, and a person never lays the same ring twice. So steal Callum's closed ease, and let my spiral's angular speed vary by about 15 percent inside the 5s, net one turn. Nobody can see the seam, and it reads as a hand, not a motor. Then steal Marisol's wet stain: the trail dries into a darker coffee-ring edge. That is the one imperfection a barista leaves on a counter.

Winner: mine, bent. Top-down, one spiral, no rings, no ticking, wrist-paced, stained trail. If Rosie sees a spinner at 120px, we lose honestly, not cutely.
