## Round 1 - Callum (Craftsperson)

Okay, I'm excited, so let me keep it short. I drew "The Pour": look straight down into a cup. A hand-wobbled spiral keeps winding in like a barista's pour-over circles, and a crema ring is born in the middle every single second. Five rings, same 5s duration, offset by one second each, so the loop closes by arithmetic, not by hope. Everything's transform and opacity, about a dozen SVG elements, and the same component scales to the splash.

Marisol, I know you want grounds, steam and a Ghibli moment. I'll fight for one tiny bloom of bubbles and not a pixel more, because it has to survive 120px. Dev, the one-second ring beat is my answer to "feels like progress". Agnes, no mascot, no smile, no checkmark. Promise.

My honest worry: it could look like a bullseye. The handle and saucer have to earn the word "coffee". And, confession, I'll say yes to every idea you throw at me and then the file bloats, so please be the people who say no to me.
