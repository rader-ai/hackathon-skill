## Round 1 - Dev (Producer)

Okay team, here's mine: "One Drop a Second." A pour-over dripper, one warm drop every second, a ripple on the crema, and the cup never fills.

Why? I sat through barista interviews, and every single one said the same thing: customers stare at the drip, and that is the one moment they relax. So the drip is the wait. We're not inventing a metaphor, we're borrowing the real one. And they hated the idea of a cup that suddenly fills up, so there is no finish, no checkmark, nothing to reset. Five equal beats tile into 5 seconds, so the loop has no seam to find.

The user story I keep coming back to is Rosie, one-handed, half-awake: "show me somebody's making it, and let me stop thinking."

Where I'm nervous: I've leaned hard on what the baristas said, and I know that is my blind spot. A drip into a cup could read as plain, and the ripple has to survive 120px. Marisol, I need you to make it beautiful without making it tiny. Agnes, tell me if it's cute rather than ownable. Callum, I'm asking for about 8 elements, and I mean it.
