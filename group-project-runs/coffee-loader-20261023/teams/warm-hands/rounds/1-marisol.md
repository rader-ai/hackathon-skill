## Round 1 - Marisol (Visionary)

Okay, listen, I'll tell you what I want. I want the *bloom*. You know that second when the hot water first lands on fresh grounds and the whole bed lifts up and exhales? That is the most beautiful thing a barista does, nobody's animated it, and it is slow by nature, which is exactly the calm Amara's panel wants. So: top-down, a hand-poured spiral, the grounds breathing, all printed like a two-ink riso poster with the second ink a hair out of register, so it feels like a person pulled it.

No cup. No steam. Agnes, you can put the knife down, there is nothing cute in it, it's a dark circle and a cream drop.

Dev, the user story is "my coffee is already being made," and a bloom says that better than a spinner.

Callum, ten shapes, please, hold me to it. I know I'll start adding chaff and filter creases and a gooseneck, and at 120px that is brown mush. Be the person who says no to me. I'll hate it and you'll be right.

Constant sweep for progress, sine breath for warmth, no finished moment, ever.
