## Round 2a - Callum (Craftsperson)

Okay, honest build-it-at-120px review, then the human thing.

**Agnes, One Pour:** the three-shape discipline is the right bones, but a rotating spiral on a dark disc IS a spinner. The 6-8 static speckles are 1px at 120px: mush. Concede that.
**Marisol, Bloom:** the riso misregistration is the best idea on the table, and it costs one offset duplicate disc. Steal it. The ripples under a dome under a drop will smear, so cut to ten shapes like you promised.
**Dev, One Drop:** a 3px elliptical ripple dies at 120px, and a dripper plus cup is the busiest silhouette of the four. The 1s beat is gold though. Steal the beat, not the dripper.
**Mine:** my five rings are a bullseye. Said it myself; it still stands.

**The human thing:** the tally. A barista counts with a marker. Five short, uneven, hand-jittered tick marks sit on the cup rim, each a slightly different length and angle (hardcoded, not generated). One inks in per second, then all five fade together at the wrap, ready to start again. Ticks are opacity plus a tiny rotate, so they stay cheap. At 120px they read as chunky dashes, and the unevenness is the barista's thumbprint. Nobody else will have someone counting for you.

**Verdict:** merge on my top-down cup. Spiral turning (Agnes's constant speed), Marisol's off-register copper disc, Dev's 1s beat done as the tally instead of rings, and cut the rings to two. About 10 elements. I vow no bubbles.
