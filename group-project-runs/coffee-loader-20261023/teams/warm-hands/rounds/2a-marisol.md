## Round 2a - Marisol (Visionary)

Okay. Honest hands up: all four of us drew a circle seen from above. Mine too. So I'll say what I'd steal, and then the human thing.

**Agnes, "One Pour":** closest to mine, and she's right about discipline. Three shapes, constant sweep, zero face. I'm stealing the closed-by-arithmetic loop and the sienna tail. Where she's weak: "slightly off-round, in exactly one place" is a footnote, not a feeling. Pace alone doesn't make a barista. Her dark disc is the spinner she fears.

**Callum, "The Pour":** the five rings are a bullseye, full stop. Handle plus saucer is a cup begging to be recognised, which is the cute thing he swore off. I'm taking his wobbled rim and his ease-that-cancels-at-the-wrap: a wrist speeds and slows, and the loop still closes.

**Dev, "One Drop":** the drip is the most generic image on the table, and a 1s tick can feel like a clock. But his barista quotes are gold: no fill-up, ever.

**Mine, stated plainly:** the bloom is still the ritual, but a dome plus a drop can read "blob". The human thing I'd add: **a coffee-ring stain, left by a wet mug on the paper.** One thin, broken, off-round ring, a thumb-smudge of copper beside it, printed a hair out of register. Nobody's loader has evidence of a previous person. It says a human worked here before you, and it costs one static path.

I concede the rings, handle, saucer, gooseneck and chaff. Ten shapes, tested at 120px first.
