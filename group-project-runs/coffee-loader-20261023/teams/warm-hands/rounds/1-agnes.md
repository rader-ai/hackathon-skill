## Round 1 - Agnes (Skeptic)

I have killed enough mascots to know how this goes: someone will propose a cup with eyelashes, we'll all say "ooh", and the judges will say "fintech with a latte". So mine is deliberately small. One Pour: a top-down bed of grounds, one crema spiral turning at constant speed, one slow bloom swell. Three shapes, transform and opacity only, loop closes by arithmetic rather than hope.

Warmth is a fair goal and I am not against it. I just want it to come from pace and one imperfect hand-drawn curve, not from a wobble and a wink. Marisol, your detail is lovely and will turn to mush at 120px, so bring it as a few static speckles, not animation. Dev, if users said they want "friendly", show me which frame says it.

Where I'm exposed: it could read as a generic spinner. Fix that with colour and the bloom, not with a face. If anyone proposes a smiling bean, I will be polite about it exactly once.
