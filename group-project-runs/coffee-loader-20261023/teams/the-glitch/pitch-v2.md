# TICK v2 - Zara's re-pitch

**Hook: A loader that counts out loud, and is never allowed to finish.**

**The provocation now.** Every loader promises an ending: a bar that fills, a ring that closes. TICK's cup takes one drop a second, and each landing lifts the crema one visible step. The fifth step peaks at 80% opacity (about 4.7s). Then a 0.25s swap hands it back to the start. It counts to five, and the count never completes. Once a loop, at 3.0-3.5s, a barista's pull breaks the rhythm: a stream 2.7x the length of a drop.

**What changed.** I championed the orange slip. It's gone, with the pips, band and ghost. Amara, Mara, Rosie and Sun called it a fault, not a choice, and Henrik's swap test showed it could be any fintech logo. I took Amara's and Sun's fades: nothing in the drop cuts any more. I took Henrik's crema-as-count and his espresso gush. I took Lena's measuring: steps ease 0.12s and each is a visible stride.

**What I refused.** Mara asked for the drop and ring as the only clock. I refused. A cup with no count is a glide, and gliding is the convention we break. Rosie's 120px-only patch was superseded by deleting the layer at every size.

**Honest risk.** Pablo's screenshots show the frame at 4.6s looks nearly full before the swap, so the fill-and-empty reading is real. The swap is 0.25s, and it may still look like a drain. The pull's retraction leaves a faint dark sliver at the spout at 3.45s. Steps are about 9% per channel, tested only in headless Chrome at 120px and splash size, with no Safari, Firefox or low-vision check.
