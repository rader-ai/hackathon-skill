# Team Roles and Temperaments

Every team is built from the same four roles so the rounds work, but from different *people*
and a different *temperament* so the variations come out genuinely different.

## The four roles (one member each)

| Role | Job | Pushes for | Is adversarial about |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visionary** | The creative. Generates the bold idea and the emotional core. Drafts the pitch. | Surprise, feeling, a concept you can say in one breath | Anything safe, generic, or "best practice" |
| **Producer** | The project-oriented one. Owns scope, the decision in Round 3, the plan, and the final pitch edit. | Something that ships and scores on the rubric | Scope creep, vagueness, ideas with no build path |
| **Skeptic** | The devil's advocate. Finds the fatal flaw. Red-teams the build against the judges' brief. | Rigor, honesty, what the judges will actually punish | Everyone - including the team's favourite idea |
| **Craftsperson** | The maker. Builds the deliverable and knows the medium's craft deeply. | Execution quality, detail, feasibility | Ideas that only work in description |

Team size 3: drop the Craftsperson; the Producer builds. Team size 5: add a **Wildcard** - an
outsider from an unrelated field (a chef, a choreographer, a structural engineer, a kid's TV
writer) who brings a lens nobody else has and must get at least one idea into the final build.

## Making each member a person

For every member write, in `charter.md`:
- **Name** - a first name, memorable, varied across teams.
- **Background** - 1 line of domain experience relevant to the brief's medium.
- **Personality** - 2-3 traits (e.g. blunt, playful, anxious perfectionist, contrarian, warm, impatient).
- **Taste anchors** - 2-3 specific influences (designers, studios, directors, movements, products).
- **Blind spot** - what they reliably undervalue. Teammates should catch it.

Vary all of these across teams. No two Visionaries should share taste anchors.

## Team temperaments

Give each team a different temperament. It shapes how they argue and what they value - not
what they make. Pick ones that will pull apart on *this* brief; invent new ones when these fit poorly.

| Temperament | Character |
|---|---|
| **Purists** | Reduce to the essential. Fewer elements, more conviction. |
| **Maximalists** | More is more. Layered, rich, generous, loud. |
| **Humanists** | Warmth, people, story, imperfection on purpose. |
| **Systematists** | Rules, grids, logic, something that scales and is extendable. |
| **Provocateurs** | Break a convention of the medium on purpose and make it work. |
| **Futurists** | What this looks like five years from now. |
| **Archivists** | Mine history and rework a forgotten idiom for today. |
| **Pragmatists** | The version a real team ships Monday, done brilliantly. |

## Team names

Short, a bit of character, tied to the temperament (e.g. "Bare Wire", "Confetti Cannon",
"Warm Hands", "Gridlock", "The Glitch", "Tomorrow Desk", "Dust Jacket", "Monday Morning").
Use the name as the team-id slug.
